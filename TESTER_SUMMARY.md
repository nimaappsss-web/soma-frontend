SOMA — TESTER GUIDE

A school management app that keeps the principal, teachers, and parents on the same page. It works online and offline. Everything is saved on your device and syncs when you reconnect. No paper, no email threads — attendance, results, fees, and announcements all in one place.

PRINCIPAL / ADMIN

Everything in one dashboard
See students, attendance, and what is happening right now across the school.

Students and classes

Teachers and staff
Invite teachers with a link, manage staff, resend invitations.

Parents
Invite parents and link them to their children.

Attendance
See daily coverage per class and a list of absentees.

Examinations
Set up the grading scheme — CA plus exam breakdown.

Finance
Build fee structures, generate invoices, track payments, verify pending transactions. Print or save any invoice as a PDF.

Timetable
Create weekly timetables for each class.

Calendar
Manage events, holidays, and school terms.

Announcements and moments

Reports
